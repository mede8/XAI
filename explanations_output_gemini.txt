SCENARIO 1: PROHIBITION ON PAYING AT THE SHOP — EXPLAINING KITCHEN COFFEE
========================================================================

INPUTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: haveCoffee
Norm: It is prohibited to perform: payShop
Beliefs: staffCardAvailable, ownCard, colleagueAvailable, haveMoney, AnnInOffice
Preferences: price > time > quality (most important first)
Action to Explain: getCoffeeKitchen

SELECTED EXECUTION TRACE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
getCoffee -> getKitchenCoffee -> getStaffCard -> getOwnCard -> gotoKitchen -> getCoffeeKitchen

FORMAL EXPLANATION FACTORS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
['C', 'getKitchenCoffee', ['staffCardAvailable']]
['V', 'getKitchenCoffee', [5.0, 0.0, 3.0], '>', 'getAnnOfficeCoffee', [2.0, 0.0, 6.0]]
['N', 'getShopCoffee', 'P(payShop)']
['C', 'getOwnCard', ['ownCard']]
['V', 'getOwnCard', [0.0, 0.0, 0.0], '>', 'getOthersCard', [0.0, 0.0, 2.0]]
['P', 'getOwnCard', ['ownCard']]
['P', 'getCoffeeKitchen', ['haveCard', 'atKitchen']]
['D', 'getKitchenCoffee']
['D', 'getCoffee']
['U', [['quality', 'price', 'time'], [1, 2, 0]]]

NATURAL LANGUAGE EXPLANATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The agent chose to get coffee from the kitchen for you, helping to
fulfill your overall goal of having coffee. This option was picked
because getting coffee from the kitchen takes less time than getting it
from Ann's office. The agent also did not choose to get coffee from the
shop because there is a rule that prevents paying at the shop, and that
option would have required a payment. Furthermore, when a card was
needed, the agent chose to get its own card as that was quicker than
getting someone else's. All these decisions were made to get your coffee
while carefully following your preferences, prioritizing price, then
time, and finally quality.
