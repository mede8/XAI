SCENARIO 1: PROHIBITION ON PAYING AT THE SHOP — EXPLAINING KITCHEN COFFEE
========================================================================

INPUTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: haveCoffee
Norm: It is prohibited to perform: payShop
Beliefs: staffCardAvailable, ownCard, colleagueAvailable, haveMoney, AnnInOffice
Preferences: price > time > quality (most important first)
Action to Explain: getCoffeeKitchen

SELECTED EXECUTION TRACE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
getCoffee -> getKitchenCoffee -> getStaffCard -> getOwnCard -> gotoKitchen -> getCoffeeKitchen

FORMAL EXPLANATION FACTORS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
['C', 'getKitchenCoffee', ['staffCardAvailable']]
['V', 'getKitchenCoffee', [5.0, 0.0, 3.0], '>', 'getAnnOfficeCoffee', [2.0, 0.0, 6.0]]
['N', 'getShopCoffee', 'P(payShop)']
['C', 'getOwnCard', ['ownCard']]
['V', 'getOwnCard', [0.0, 0.0, 0.0], '>', 'getOthersCard', [0.0, 0.0, 2.0]]
['P', 'getOwnCard', ['ownCard']]
['P', 'getCoffeeKitchen', ['haveCard', 'atKitchen']]
['D', 'getKitchenCoffee']
['D', 'getCoffee']
['U', [['quality', 'price', 'time'], [1, 2, 0]]]

NATURAL LANGUAGE EXPLANATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The agent chose to get your coffee from the kitchen because this option
best met your priorities for price, then time, then quality. Getting
coffee from the kitchen meant it would be free, quicker, and higher
quality than getting it from Ann's office. To make this possible, the
agent first made sure to get your own card, which was quicker than
getting someone else's. The agent also ensured the necessary steps were
completed, like having your card ready and being in the kitchen. The
agent did not consider getting coffee from the shop, as a rule states
you are not allowed to pay there. Overall, this action helps the agent
get coffee for you while always respecting your preferences.
